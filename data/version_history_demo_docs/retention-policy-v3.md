# Acme Retention Policy (v3.0, scheduled)

From 2027 Acme keeps processed customer documents for 180 days and compliance evidence for 730 days.

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Processed customer documents | 180 days |
| Compliance evidence | 730 days |
